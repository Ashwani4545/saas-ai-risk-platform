# Credit Score Policy

Credit score is one of the strongest single predictors of default risk in
our model and carries the heaviest weight in manual review guidelines.

Applicants with a credit score above 720 are generally considered
low-risk and are eligible for standard terms without additional review.

Applicants scoring between 650 and 720 fall into a medium-risk band. These
applications should be reviewed alongside transaction frequency and
account age before a final decision - a strong transaction history can
offset a middling credit score.

Applicants scoring below 650 are high-risk by policy and require manual
underwriter sign-off regardless of what the automated model outputs. A
low credit score combined with any dispute history should be treated as
a strong signal against approval.

Credit score alone should never be the sole basis for an adverse decision
- it must be considered alongside dispute history, account age, and
transaction patterns, and the specific factors must be disclosed to the
applicant per adverse action notice requirements.
