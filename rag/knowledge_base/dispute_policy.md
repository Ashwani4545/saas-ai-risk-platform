# Dispute History Policy

Number of disputes is a strong predictor of future default risk in our
model - customers with even one prior dispute default at a meaningfully
higher rate than those with none.

Zero disputes on record is treated as a neutral-to-positive signal and
does not by itself trigger any additional review.

One dispute in the last 12 months requires a note in the underwriting
file but does not automatically block approval. Consider the nature of
the dispute if it is available - billing disputes are treated less
severely than disputes involving non-payment.

Two or more disputes in the last 12 months is treated as a high-risk
signal by policy. Combined with a credit score below 650, this
combination should default to manual review rather than automated
approval, even if the model's raw risk score is borderline.

Disputes older than 24 months should be weighted less heavily than
recent disputes, since they are less predictive of current risk than a
recent pattern of disputes.
